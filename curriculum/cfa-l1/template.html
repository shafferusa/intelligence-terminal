From Day 0. On the BA II Plus, what does P/Y need to be for an annual problem?

  • 12 is the factory setting, and the most common silent error: with P/Y = 12 an annual rate is treated as monthly.

  • Right: one payment (and one compounding) per year.

  • It changes every answer: I/Y is divided by P/Y.

Why

Set P/Y = 1 once and leave it; adjust N and I/Y yourself for other periods.

The idea

Why it works

The formula built up step by step, so nothing has to be taken on trust.

FV = PV × (1 + r)N

FV
future value
PV
present value (today’s amount)
r
interest rate per period, as a decimal
N
number of periods
Memorize
  • FV = PV × (1 + r)N
  • PV = FV / (1 + r)N

Problem. You invest $1,000 at 5% a year for 10 years. What is it worth at the end?

  1. Clear the old numbers

    BA II Plus 2ND CLR TVM
    TI-84 Plus APPS 1:Finance 1:TVM Solver

  2. Enter the inputs

    BA II Plus 10 N 5 I/Y 1000 +|− PV 0 PMT
    TI-84 Plus N=10, I%=5, PV=−1000, PMT=0, P/Y=1, C/Y=1, PMT: END (type (−) for the minus sign)

  3. Solve for FV

    BA II Plus CPT FV
    TI-84 Plus cursor on FV=, then ALPHA ENTER

    Display: FV = 1,628.89 (BA II Plus, 2 decimals) / FV=1628.894627 (TI-84)

Answer: $1,628.89.
