How it works

Take the days in order, roughly one a day: each is 60 to 120 minutes, heavier where the exam is heavier. Every edition teaches from first principles, then separates what to understand, what to memorize, the exam traps, and the trader connection. Read the matching Study Guide chapter before or after for a second pass.

Each edition ends with a quiz, and days 26, 28 and 30 are full 75-question practice exams. Nothing is revealed until you tap Grade my answers; then every question shows the rule and why each wrong answer is wrong. Tag each miss as rule-based, conceptual or careless.

Your results build the progress page: a Strong / Adequate / Weak / Very Weak rating for every topic, a running error log, and a review deck that brings each missed question back about 1, 3 and 7 days later until you get it right twice. Use the review deck every day, and lean on it hardest on days 24–29.

Progress is saved in this browser on this phone. Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate copies, so pick one (the progress page can copy a backup between them).

The exam
  • 75 scored questions plus 5 unscored; 1 hour 45 minutes; passing score 70.
  • Capital markets 16% · Products and their risks 44% · Trading, accounts and prohibited activities 31% · Regulatory framework 9%.
