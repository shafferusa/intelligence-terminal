Set up your calculators once

Exam rule Only the TI BA II Plus (or BA II Plus Professional) and the HP 12C family are allowed in the exam room. The TI-84 Plus is not, and a result earned with an unapproved calculator is voided. The lessons show both so you can learn on either, but sit the exam with the BA II Plus.
BA II Plus
  1. Payments per year to 1 (it ships set to 12, the most common silent error): 2ND P/Y 1 ENTER 2ND QUIT. Setting P/Y sets C/Y to the same value.
  2. Show four decimals: 2ND FORMAT 4 ENTER 2ND QUIT.
  3. Normal order of operations (AOS): 2ND FORMAT, press ↓ until the display shows Chn or AOS, press 2ND SET until it shows AOS, then 2ND QUIT.
  4. Before every new problem: 2ND CLR TVM for time value, or CF 2ND CLR WORK for cash flows.
TI-84 Plus
  1. The finance tools are in APPS 1:Finance; 1:TVM Solver is the time-value screen.
  2. In the TVM Solver set P/Y=1 and C/Y=1 unless the problem says otherwise, and PMT: END.
  3. Four decimals: MODE, move to the row reading FLOAT 0123456789, choose 4, ENTER, then 2nd MODE (QUIT).
  4. To solve in the TVM Solver, move the cursor to the unknown and press ALPHA ENTER (SOLVE).

Both calculators use the same sign rule: money you pay out is negative, money you receive is positive.
